What it does

Four things, done without asking you to set anything up.

  • It decides what's urgent

    Each assignment is scored on its due date, what's riding on it and how much work it needs. A small language model running on your phone reads the title to tell an exam from a worksheet from a project.

  • It plans around your day

    Work is split into sessions of about an hour, spread out so you finish a day early, and fitted between your calendar events. Drag a session somewhere else and the rest of the plan rearranges itself around it.

  • It works without a signal

    Assignments, your plan and your reminders are all saved on the phone. It only goes online when you sign in or ask for fresh data.

  • Cling stays with you

    A little star can float over your other apps. Tap it to ask what's next or to plan study time, and drag it to the bottom of the screen to send it away.

Installing

  1. Download the APK on your phone.
  2. Open it. If Android asks, allow installs from this source (Settings, then Install unknown apps).
