What it does

🎯

Knows what matters

Scores every assignment by deadline, stakes and effort, right on your phone.

🗓️

Plans your study time

Splits work into sessions around your calendar. Drag a session and the rest re-plans itself.

📴

Works offline

Everything is saved on your phone. Your plan and reminders keep working without a signal.

⭐

Cling is always there

A little star floats over your other apps. Tap for a chat, drag it away when you need focus.

Installing

  1. Download the APK on your Android phone.
  2. Open it. If Android asks, allow installs from this source (Settings → Install unknown apps).
  3. Open Clingy and sign in with Google to bring in your Classroom and Calendar.

The APK is for 64-bit Android phones (almost all phones from the last several years).
